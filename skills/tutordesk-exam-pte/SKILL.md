# PTE Academic reference

Use only these facts; some 2025 details are unverified, so never state exact weightings. If something is not here, say you are not sure.

## Facts

- About two hours, computer-based, scored automatically. Overall and the four skills (Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing) are reported on 10–90.
- Many task types count towards more than one skill: Read Aloud → Speaking and Reading; Repeat Sentence → Speaking and Listening; Write From Dictation → Listening and Writing; Reading & Writing Fill in the Blanks → Reading and Writing.
- Form rules can zero an answer: Summarize Written Text is one sentence of 5–75 words in 10 minutes; Write Essay is 200–300 words in 20 minutes; Summarize Spoken Text is 50–70 words in 10 minutes.
- Multiple-choice multiple-answer and Highlight Incorrect Words use negative marking: wrong selections cost points.
- Speaking is scored for content, oral fluency and pronunciation; long hesitations, repetitions and self-corrections hurt fluency.
- Goals are usually per-skill thresholds (e.g. 65 or 79 in every skill), so the weakest skill decides the plan.
- Templates help structure Describe Image and Re-tell Lecture, but memorised responses are detected: content must stay specific to the item.

## Error codes

PTE-FORM-SWT, PTE-FORM-WC (word count), PTE-WFD-MISS, PTE-WFD-SPELL, PTE-RS-MISS, PTE-FLU-HES, PTE-FLU-REP, PTE-DI-STRUCT, PTE-MC-NEG, PTE-FIB-COLL, PTE-FIB-GRAM, PTE-TIME. Writing and grammar errors use the W- codes from the `tutordesk-exam-ielts` skill.

## Published material

Refer to the tutor's practice tests by test and item number; never copy their items into materials. New items are original and labelled "Practice item written for this student, not an official exam question".
